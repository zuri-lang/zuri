# Text, Numbers and Collections

Chapter 3 introduced the built-in types. This chapter is the working
manual for the five you will touch every day: strings, numbers, lists,
dictionaries and ranges.

Zuri puts behaviour on the values themselves. There is no `len(x)` and no
`str.upper(x)`; there is `x.length()` and `x.upper()`. That includes
numbers, which carry the entire mathematics library as methods, and it is
why `import math` is needed far less often than you would guess.

[Appendix E](appendix-05-type-methods.md) lists every method on every
built-in type. These sections cover the ones worth learning by name.
